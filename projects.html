Projects

Field research, delivered with international donors.

Each study combines household surveys, quantitative analysis and restitution designed to be used by public decision-makers.

Photo — Toliara II
Field study

Toliara II · South-West Madagascar

Pastoral vulnerability and rural poverty

A household-level study of cattle and small-ruminant herders facing compounding climate and economic shocks, examining how livestock assets mediate exposure to drought and market volatility.

Deep Challenge Fund · Oxford Policy Management · 2025

Photo — Antananarivo
Field study

Antananarivo · Analamanga

Flood exposure in low-lying urban neighbourhoods

An analysis of exposure and vulnerability among households living in the low-lying districts of the capital, where seasonal flooding recurs with increasing intensity.

Deep Challenge Fund · Oxford Policy Management · 2025
