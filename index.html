Applied research & policy analysis

Understanding vulnerability to inform public action.

"Rigour in the service of those least heard."

NGO LEFONA is an independent research organisation, founded and led by young Malagasy professionals. We study how households in Madagascar absorb climate and economic shocks — and what public policy can do about it.

What we do

Four areas of work, one standard of evidence.

From data collection in the field to recommendations on a minister's desk.

Our expertise in detail
01

Field studies and household surveys

Quantitative and qualitative data collection from households and local institutions, with sampling protocols and quality controls aligned with international donor requirements.

02

Public policy analysis

Translating research findings into operational recommendations, with deliverables designed to be used directly by public decision-makers.

03

Donor-funded project coordination

Day-to-day management of internationally funded research, working alongside the Malagasy administration and partner research teams.

04

Academic supervision and training

Supervision of master's dissertations and delivery of methodology training, in partnership with Malagasy universities.
