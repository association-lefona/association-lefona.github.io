Our expertise

A team trained to international donor standards.

Rigorous methodology, actionable restitution, strong local roots.

— 01

Field studies & surveys

Collection and analysis of quantitative and qualitative data from households and local institutions, using sampling protocols aligned with international donor standards.

— 02

Public policy advice

Translating research findings into operational recommendations for public decision-makers, with clear deliverables and actionable restitution.

— 03

Donor coordination

Management of internationally funded projects, working directly with the Malagasy administration and with partner research teams.

— 04

Academic supervision

Supervision of master's dissertations and delivery of training sessions, in partnership with Malagasy universities.
